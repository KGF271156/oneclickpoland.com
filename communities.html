Communities built to last

Polish parishes & centres

Polish-language Masses, community halls and cultural centres, from the Polish Social and Cultural Association (POSK) in London to the Polish parishes of Chicago and Toronto.

Saturday schools

Szkoły sobotnie teach Polish language, history and tradition to children born abroad. A directory is planned.

Folk dance & music groups

Zespoły: ensembles dancing the krakowiak, oberek and polonaise in regional costume.

Scouts & youth

Polish scouting (harcerstwo) has a long tradition abroad and is often the first Polish community young people meet.

Heritage & veterans

Associations remembering Polish airmen, soldiers and refugees of the Second World War, and the Poles who built communities after 1945.

Professionals & newcomers

Networks for those who moved after 2004, from nurses and builders to IT specialists and entrepreneurs.
