Throughout the year

National dates worth knowing

Easter & Śmigus-dyngus

March–April: baskets of food are blessed on Holy Saturday, and Easter Monday is a day of water-throwing and fun.

3 May: Constitution Day

Marks Europe's first modern written constitution, adopted in 1791. Parades and flags in Polish communities.

1 November: All Saints'

Poles travel home to light candles on family graves. The cemeteries glow at night; one of the most moving days of the year.

11 November: Independence Day

Marks the restoration of independence in 1918, with marches, concerts and the national colours everywhere.

Wigilia: 24 December

The Christmas Eve supper begins when the first star appears, with twelve dishes and an empty place at the table.
