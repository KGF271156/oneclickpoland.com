MADE IN POLAND

Gaming, made in Poland.

The Witcher, Cyberpunk, This War of Mine, Dying Light: Poland is one of the great games nations, and its players are among the most connected anywhere.

Poland's studios

A national success story

CD Projekt Red

Warsaw studio behind The Witcher series and Cyberpunk 2077. Its parent company also owns the storefront GOG.com.

Techland

Wrocław studio known for Dying Light, a zombie survival series played worldwide.

11 bit studios

Warsaw studio behind This War of Mine and Frostpunk, games praised for their serious themes.

Bloober Team

Kraków horror specialists behind Layers of Fear and the remake of Silent Hill 2.

People Can Fly

Warsaw studio known for Bulletstorm and work with other major publishers.

The indie scene

Hundreds of small teams across Poland make games; many are celebrated at events in Kraków and Poznań.

Meet and play

Events worth knowing

Poznań Game Arena

Poland's biggest consumer gaming show, held in Poznań each autumn.

Digital Dragons

A games industry conference held in Kraków for developers from around Europe.

Gamescom & more

Polish studios are a fixture at Gamescom in Cologne and other major European shows.
