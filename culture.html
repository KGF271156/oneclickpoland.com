POLAND, BEYOND THE POSTCARD

Culture that travels.

From Chopin to cabaret, from folk costume to Wigilia: the threads that make Polish culture recognisable anywhere in the world.

Discover

What carries abroad

Language & dialect

Polish is spoken by around forty million people, and its regional varieties, from Silesian to Kashubian and the Highlander (Górale) speech of the Tatras, carry on in families abroad.

Music

Chopin's mazurkas and polonaises, the folk music of the Podhale highlands, Polish jazz and the Opole song festival: music is how many Poles stay close to home.

Faith & tradition

Wigilia (Christmas Eve supper), the blessing of Easter baskets (Święconka), name days and pilgrimages to Częstochowa are kept alive in Polish parishes around the world.

Literature & film

Five Poles have won the Nobel Prize in Literature, and the Polish Film School produced directors such as Wajda, Polański and Kieślowski.

Folk art & craft

Paper cut-outs (wycinanki), Bolesławiec pottery, amber from the Baltic and hand-painted Easter eggs (pisanki) are loved far beyond Poland.

Poland abroad

Culture kept alive a generation on

Polish parishes, Saturday schools, folk dance ensembles and scout troops have kept Polish identity alive abroad for generations, from the Polonia of Chicago to the post-war communities of Britain and the newer communities that have grown since 2004.
