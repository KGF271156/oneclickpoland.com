Kibic: sport, Polish style.

A kibic is a fan, and Polish fans are among the most passionate anywhere. Football, volleyball, ski jumping and speedway: Polish sport for wherever you're watching from.

What Poland watches

Football (piłka nożna)

The Ekstraklasa has fierce rivalries: Legia vs Lech, Wisła vs Cracovia, Lechia vs Arka. The national team finished third at the 1974 and 1982 World Cups, and Robert Lewandowski is the all-time top scorer.

Volleyball (siatkówka)

Poland is a global volleyball power. The men's team won the World Championship in 2014 and 2018, and packed halls follow the PlusLiga.

Ski jumping & winter sport

From Adam Małysz to Kamil Stoch, ski jumping stops the nation. Zakopane hosts a World Cup event every January.

Speedway (żużel)

Poland has the world's strongest speedway league, with big crowds in Toruń, Wrocław, Gorzów, Zielona Góra and Leszno.

Tennis & athletics

Iga Świątek has been world number one and a multiple Grand Slam champion; Poland also has a proud record in the hammer throw and middle-distance running.
